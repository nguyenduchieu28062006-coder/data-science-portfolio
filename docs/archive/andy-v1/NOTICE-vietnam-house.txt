Dataset: Real Estate in Vietnam, publisher andyvo1009, version 1.
Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/andyvo1009/real-estate-in-vietnam
Input: sale_real_estate.csv (42,257 records), Apache-2.0 declared by publisher.
Published period: February 2025, according to publisher description.
No trustworthy per-listing timestamps; no independent upstream-rights verification.
Original raw CSV retained outside the public site; its SHA-256 is in model metadata.

Modifications by this portfolio project: invalid/ambiguous records and duplicates
excluded, province aliases normalized, unknown room counts imputed from training
only, fitted regression exported as JSON, and three real source examples included.
Original location names and source IDs are retained for those examples.
This is an asking-price portfolio model, not verified completed transactions.
