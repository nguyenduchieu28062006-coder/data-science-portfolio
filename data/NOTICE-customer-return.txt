customer-return-cohorts.csv is an adaptation: cleaned transactions aggregated
into customer snapshots; source IDs replaced with deterministic pseudonymous
keys. Future valid purchases define a 60-day binary repeat-purchase target.
customer-return-parity-fixtures.json contains anonymized feature vectors and
Python model probabilities, not customer identifiers or transaction details.
See docs/customer-return-methodology.md and docs/customer-return-model-report.json
for exact exclusions, time windows, leakage checks and model evaluation.

The raw XLSX is not bundled with the website. Download the official source to
reproduce training with scripts/train-customer-return.py. No other project's
datasets or model artifacts are used or modified.
